## Design Context

### Scope
This design context applies only to the `/rendered` routes and their supporting components, styles, and assets.

The main site experience on `/` and all other non-`/rendered` routes remains the hand-drawn, canvas-based "work in progress" world. That canvas language is already settled and should not inherit the aesthetic decisions defined below.

The full spec (reference study, tokens, components, accessibility rules, decisions) is `docs/rendered-design.md`. Read it before changing the rendered design.

### Users
The rendered experience serves four overlapping audiences:

- Hiring managers and recruiters evaluating overall quality and distinctiveness
- Design leads and founders assessing product thinking, craft, and taste
- Peers in design who will notice nuance, originality, and execution quality
- Potential clients looking for confidence, credibility, and a reason to get in touch

Their job is not only to review work samples, but to understand Prerita's point of view as a designer. The page should get out of the way, load instantly, and make the work and the person easy to read.

### Brand Personality
Curious, sharp, quietly personal. The personality lives in the writing, the case studies and small details, not in decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
The rendered pages are the polished "build" of the hand-drawn sketch: the same content, compiled into something precise and calm. The direction is **minimal and typographic**, modeled on alexkehr.com (user's decision, replacing an earlier bolder, more expressive direction):

- Light mode only
- One narrow column (540px, 492px of text) on every page
- One sans family (Geist) plus one mono (Geist Mono) for labels and small details. No serif, no italics
- Hierarchy from size and a grey ramp, not from boxes, rules or uppercase
- Almost no color: real logos and screenshots are the only decoration
- Navigation is one small floating bar: top on desktop, bottom on phones
- Motion is small and always says something (route change, current page, what a link does, copied). CSS only, transform and opacity, reduced motion respected. See docs/rendered-design.md 3.8

Anti-direction:

- No dark-mode portfolio aesthetic
- No decorative illustration, grain, gradients, heavy shadows or card grids
- No text lighter than `#737373` (WCAG AA); no gray-on-color text

### Design Principles
1. Content first. Space, size and grey value do the work of boxes and borders.
2. One width, one rhythm. Every page uses the same column and the same spacing scale.
3. Accessible by default: AA contrast, visible focus, landmarks, a skip link, 44px targets, and no hidden or clamped text.
4. Fast by default: static pages, self-hosted fonts, sized images, nothing that shifts while loading.
5. Make curiosity lead to contact: the "Let's talk" link is always one tap away.
